# F-0007: Shortlisted Manufacturing Partners (CanineCare Pro)

This document identifies potential contract manufacturing (CM) partners with specialized expertise in waterproof electronics and silicone overmolding for the pet care IoT market.

| Partner Name | Region | Specialized Expertise | Recommendation Level |

| **SiliconePlus** | China | High-volume manufacturing of IP67/IP68 silicone overmolded consumer electronics. | **High** (Scale focus) |
| **P1 Technologies** | USA (CT) | End-to-end electromechanical assembly and custom overmolding. | **Medium** |
| **Konark Silicones**| India/Global | Focuses on material bonding between silicone and plastic/metal substrates. | **Medium** |

## 2. Selection Criteria for CanineCare Pro
- **Waterproof Rating:** Must guarantee IPX7 or better for the full assembly.
- **Material Safety:** Verification of medical-grade/food-grade non-toxic silicone sourcing.
- **Chew Resistance:** Experience with reinforced internal frames or high-durometer silicone.
- **IoT Integration:** Ability to handle PCBA sourcing and Bluetooth testing in-house.
