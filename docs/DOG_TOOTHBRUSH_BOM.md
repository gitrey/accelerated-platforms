## 4. Sourcing Strategy
- **MCU/BLE:** Standardizing on Nordic Semiconductor or Espressif for low-power Bluetooth stability.
- **Motor:** Sourcing from specialized ultrasonic transducer manufacturers in Shenzhen or Japan.
- **Silicone:** Partnering with medical-grade polymer suppliers (e.g., Dow Corning, Wacker).
