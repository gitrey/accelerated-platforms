# Hardware Specifications: CanineCare Pro (Smart Dog Toothbrush)

This document defines the hardware specifications for the ultrasonic motor and battery systems for the CanineCare Pro smart toothbrush.

## 1. Ultrasonic Motor Specifications

To ensure effective plaque removal without distressing the dog with loud mechanical noise or aggressive physical movement, we utilize a piezoelectric ultrasonic transducer.

| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Operating Frequency** | 1.6 MHz (96,000,000 pulses/min) | Standard ultrasonic frequency for dental plaque removal. |
| **Output Amplitude** | 0.02 mm - 0.05 mm | Low-amplitude vibration to avoid tissue damage. |
| **Transducer Type** | Piezoelectric Ceramic | Highly efficient, compact, and generates minimal heat. |
| **Sonic Assist (Optional)** | 20,000 - 40,000 VPM | Low-frequency sonic vibration for physical debris removal. |
| **Noise Level** | < 35 dB | Ultra-quiet operation to avoid scaring dogs with sensitive hearing. |

## 2. Battery & Power Management

The device must be waterproof and provide enough capacity for at least two weeks of use (twice daily brushing).

| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Battery Type** | Lithium-ion (Li-ion) | Rechargeable, lightweight. |
| **Capacity** | 1,000 mAh | Sufficient for approx. 30 days of standby or 14 days of active use. |
| **Voltage** | 3.7V | Standard Li-ion nominal voltage. |
| **Charging Method** | Qi-Compatible Inductive Charging | Fully sealed unit for maximum waterproofing (IPX7). |
| **Charging Time** | 4 - 6 Hours | From 0% to 100%. |
| **Protection Circuit** | Overcharge, Over-discharge, Thermal | Essential for safety in a device used near pets. |

## 3. Communication Module

| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Connectivity** | Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) 5.2 | High efficiency, low power consumption for app syncing. |
| **Antenna Type** | Internal PCB Antenna | Compact and protected within the silicone housing. |

## 4. Housing & Safety

| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Waterproofing** | IPX7 | Can be submerged in 1m of water for 30 minutes. |
| **Material** | Medical-grade Silicone / BPA-free ABS | Chew-resistant and non-toxic. |
| **Thermal Limit** | < 42°C (Surface Temperature) | To prevent discomfort in the dog's mouth during prolonged use. |
