# F-0007: Smart Ultrasonic Toothbrush for Dogs (CanineCare Pro)

- **Type:** Feature
- **Status:** Draft
- **Priority:** P2
- **JIRA ID:** TBD

## Problem
Maintaining dental hygiene in dogs is challenging. Manual brushing is often stressful for the animal and difficult for the owner, leading to plaque buildup, periodontal disease, and expensive veterinary dental cleanings.

## Requirements
1. **Ergonomic & Multi-Size Support:** Design brush heads suitable for toy, medium, and large breeds.
2. **Automated Ultrasonic Cleaning:** High-frequency vibration to remove plaque without requiring the physical "scrubbing" motion that stresses dogs.
3. **Incentive Integration:** Built-in reservoir for dog-friendly toothpaste or liquid treats to encourage the dog to accept the brush.
4. **IoT Connectivity:** Bluetooth integration with a mobile app to track brushing duration, frequency, and brush head replacement cycles.
5. **Durability & Safety:** Made from medical-grade, chew-resistant, non-toxic silicone.

## Manufacturing & Supply Chain Plan
1. **Design for Manufacturing (DFM):** Refine the industrial design to optimize for high-volume injection molding (handle) and overmolding (silicone heads).
2. **Bill of Materials (BOM) Sourcing:**
   - **Electronics:** Source ultrasonic transducers, Bluetooth LE modules, and rechargeable Li-ion batteries from certified electronics manufacturers.
   - **Materials:** Procure medical-grade, BPA-free, non-toxic silicone and chew-resistant ABS plastics.
3. **Prototyping Phases:**
   - **Alpha:** 3D-printed enclosures with breadboard electronics to test ultrasonic frequency efficacy.
   - **Beta:** CNC-machined prototypes with custom PCBs for ergonomic and battery life testing.
4. **Tooling & Mass Production:**
   - Develop steel molds for the handle and interchangeable heads.
   - Partner with a contract manufacturer (CM) experienced in waterproof consumer electronics (IPX7 rating).
5. **Quality Assurance (QA) & Testing:**
   - Implement rigorous chew-resistance testing using mechanical "bite" simulators.
   - 100% water-immersion testing for every unit.
6. **Regulatory Certification:**
   - Obtain FCC/CE certification for Bluetooth and electronic components.
   - Ensure material safety compliance (FDA/GRAS equivalent for pet products).
