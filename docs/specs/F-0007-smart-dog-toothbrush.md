# F-0007: Smart Ultrasonic Toothbrush for Dogs (CanineCare Pro)

- **Type:** Feature
- **Status:** Draft
- **Priority:** P2
- **JIRA ID:** TBD

## Problem
Maintaining dental hygiene in dogs is challenging. Manual brushing is often stressful for the animal and difficult for the owner, leading to plaque buildup, periodontal disease, and expensive veterinary dental cleanings.

## Requirements
1. **Ergonomic & Multi-Size Support:** Design brush heads suitable for toy, medium, and large breeds.
2. **Automated Ultrasonic Cleaning:** High-frequency vibration to remove plaque without requiring the physical "scrubbing" motion that stresses dogs.
3. **Incentive Integration:** Built-in reservoir for dog-friendly toothpaste or liquid treats to encourage the dog to accept the brush.
4. **IoT Connectivity:** Bluetooth integration with a mobile app to track brushing duration, frequency, and brush head replacement cycles.
5. **Durability & Safety:** Made from medical-grade, chew-resistant, non-toxic silicone.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Industrial design concept for the handle and interchangeable heads completed.
- [x] Prototype for the ultrasonic motor and battery housing defined.
- [x] Initial schema for the companion tracking app developed.
