# CanineCare Pro: Industrial Design Concepts

## Overview
The CanineCare Pro is a smart ultrasonic toothbrush designed specifically for dogs. It focuses on reducing stress for the animal while maximizing dental hygiene efficiency through ultrasonic technology and incentive-based design.

## 1. Handle Design
The handle is the core unit containing the ultrasonic motor, battery, and Bluetooth module.

- **Form Factor:** Ergonomic, slightly curved cylindrical shape for easy grip by human owners.
- **Material:** High-grip, medical-grade, non-toxic silicone over-mold on a durable ABS plastic internal frame.
- **Controls:**
    - Single multi-function button (Power/Mode).
    - LED indicators for battery life and connectivity status.
- **Charging:** Magnetic induction charging base to ensure water resistance (IPX7 rated).

## 2. Multi-Size Brush Heads
Interchangeable brush heads tailored to different dog sizes.

### Concept A: The "Toy" Breed Head (Small)
- **Dimensions:** 15mm x 10mm brushing surface.
- **Shape:** Ultra-slim profile to fit into small mouths comfortably.
- **Bristles:** Extra soft, dense silicone nubs for gentle plaque removal.

### Concept B: The "Medium" Breed Head (Standard)
- **Dimensions:** 25mm x 15mm brushing surface.
- **Shape:** Contoured head to wrap around canine teeth.
- **Bristles:** Dual-length silicone bristles for surface and interdental cleaning.

### Concept C: The "Large" Breed Head (Pro)
- **Dimensions:** 35mm x 20mm brushing surface.
- **Shape:** Robust head with reinforced neck to withstand higher bite pressure.
- **Bristles:** Firm yet flexible silicone bristles for deep cleaning.

## 3. Incentive Integration: Liquid Treat Reservoir
To encourage acceptance, the CanineCare Pro features a patented treat delivery system.

- **Mechanism:** A small, refillable reservoir integrated into the neck of the brush head.
- **Delivery:** Micro-pores in the brush head slowly release dog-friendly liquid treats (e.g., peanut butter flavored gel or poultry broth concentrate) during the ultrasonic vibration.
- **Cleaning:** The reservoir is detachable and dishwasher safe.

## 4. Ultrasonic Technology
- **Frequency:** 1.6 MHz (96,000,000 pulses per minute).
- **Function:** Breaks up plaque and tartar without the need for mechanical scrubbing, making it silent and vibration-free for the dog's comfort.

## 5. Durability & Safety
- **Chew Resistance:** The neck and head are reinforced with a flexible but bite-resistant polymer core.
- **Safety Cut-off:** Pressure sensor that stops ultrasonic vibration if the dog bites down too hard on the brush head.
- **Materials:** BPA-free, Phthalate-free, medical-grade silicone.
